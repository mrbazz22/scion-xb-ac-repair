Expansion Valve Replacement
1
Recover Refrigerant
The expansion valve is on the high-pressure side. Recover all refrigerant from the system before opening any connections.
WARNING: Never open AC lines under pressure. System must be fully evacuated.
2
Locate the Expansion Valve
On the Scion xB, the expansion valve is located at the evaporator inlet, typically behind the glove box or on the firewall side of the HVAC unit. It meters refrigerant flow into the evaporator.
3
Remove Old Valve
Disconnect the inlet (liquid line) and outlet (evaporator) lines. Remove the mounting bolts or clamp. Extract the old valve carefully.
NOTICE: Seal all openings immediately with vinyl tape or plugs.
4
Install New Valve
Install new GPD 3411860 expansion valve with new O-rings from the UAC RS2584 kit. Lubricate O-rings with ND-OIL8 before installation.
Tip: The valve is directional — check flow arrow matches refrigerant direction.
5
Reattach Lines
Connect liquid line and evaporator line. Torque to spec (typically 9-11 Nm). Use new O-rings on both connections.
